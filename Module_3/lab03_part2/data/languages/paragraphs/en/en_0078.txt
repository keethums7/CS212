Wikimedia Foundation spokesman Jay Walsh strongly rejected Sanger's accusation,[284] saying that Wikipedia did not have "material we would deem to be illegal. If we did, we would remove it."[284] Following the complaint by Sanger, Wales deleted sexual images without consulting the community. After volunteer editors argued that the decision was made hastily, Wales voluntarily gave up some powers he had held up as part of his co-founder status. He wrote in a message to the Wikimedia Foundation mailing-list that this action was "in the interest of encouraging this discussion to be about real philosophical/content issues, rather than be about me and how quickly I acted".[285] Critics, including Wikipediocracy, noticed that many of the pornographic images deleted from Wikipedia since 2010 have reappeared.[286]
