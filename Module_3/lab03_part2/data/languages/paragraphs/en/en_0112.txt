The Android app for Wikipedia was released in January 2012, to over 500,000 installs and generally positive reviews, scoring over four of a possible five in a poll of approximately 200,000 users downloading from Google.[399][400] The version for iOS was released on April 3, 2013, to similar reviews.[401] Wikipedia Zero was an initiative of the Wikimedia Foundation to expand the reach of the encyclopedia to the developing countries by partnering with mobile operators to allow free access.[402][403] It was discontinued in February 2018 due to lack of participation from mobile operators.[402]
