Academic studies of Wikipedia have shown that the average contributor to the English Wikipedia is an educated, technically inclined white male, aged 15–49, from a developed, predominantly Christian country.[270] The corresponding point of view (POV) is over-represented.[271][215] This systemic bias in editor demographic results in cultural bias, gender bias, and geographical bias on Wikipedia.[272][273] There are two broad types of bias, which are implicit (when a topic is omitted) and explicit (when a certain POV is over-represented in an article or by references).[271]
