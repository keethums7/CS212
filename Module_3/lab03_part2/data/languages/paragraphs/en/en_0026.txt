Wikipedia editors often have disagreements regarding content, which can be discussed on article Talk pages. Disputes may result in repeated competing changes to an article, known as "edit warring".[120][121] It is widely seen as a resource-consuming scenario where no useful knowledge is added,[122] and criticized as creating a competitive[123] and conflict-based editing culture associated with traditional masculine gender roles.[124][125] Research has focused on, for example, impoliteness of disputes,[126][127] the influence of rival editing camps,[128][129] the conversational structure,[130] and the shift in conflicts to a focus on sources.[131][132]
