Initially available only in English, Wikipedia has grown to over 300 languages and is one of the world's most visited websites as of 2026[update]. The English Wikipedia, with over 7 million articles, remains the largest of the editions, which together comprise more than 68 million articles and attract more than 1.5 billion unique device visits and 13 million edits per month (about five edits per second on average)  as of April 2024.[update][4] As of December 2025,[update] over 25% of Wikipedia's traffic comes from the United States, while Japan accounts for nearly 7%, and the United Kingdom, Germany, and Russia each represent around 5%.[5]
