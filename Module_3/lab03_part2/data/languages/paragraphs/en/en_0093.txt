Several MediaWiki extensions are installed to extend the functionality of the MediaWiki software.[320] In April 2005, a Lucene extension[321][322] was added to MediaWiki's built-in search and Wikipedia switched from MySQL to Lucene for searching. Lucene was later replaced by CirrusSearch which is based on Elasticsearch.[323] In July 2013, after extensive beta testing, a WYSIWYG (What You See Is What You Get) extension, VisualEditor, was opened to public use.[324][325][326] It was met with much rejection and criticism, and was described as "slow and buggy".[327] The feature was changed from opt-out to opt-in afterward.[328]
