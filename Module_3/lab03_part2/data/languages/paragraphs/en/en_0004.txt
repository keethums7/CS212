Wikipedia was launched on January 15, 2001 (sometimes referred to as "Wikipedia Day"),[21] as a single English language edition with the domain name www.wikipedia.com,[22] and was announced by Sanger on the Nupedia mailing list.[18] The name, proposed by Sanger to forestall any potential damage to the Nupedia name,[23] originated from a blend of the words wiki and encyclopedia.[24][25] Wales stated that the idea for Wikipedia was influenced by the spontaneous order ideas associated with Friedrich Hayek and the Austrian School of economics after being exposed to these ideas by the libertarian economist Mark Thornton.[26][27] In particular, Wales was influenced by Hayek's essay The Use of Knowledge in Society stating it was "central" to his thinking about "how to manage the Wikipedia project".[27] Its integral policy of "neutral point of view" arose within its first year.[28] Otherwise, there were initially relatively few rules, and it operated independently of Nupedia.[18] Bomis originally intended for it to be a for-profit business.[29]
