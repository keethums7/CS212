On January 18, 2023, Wikipedia debuted a new website redesign, called "Vector 2022".[82][83] It featured a redesigned menu bar, moving the table of contents to the left as a sidebar, and numerous changes in the locations of buttons like the language selection tool.[83][84] The update initially received backlash, most notably when editors of the Swahili Wikipedia unanimously voted to revert the changes.[82][85]
